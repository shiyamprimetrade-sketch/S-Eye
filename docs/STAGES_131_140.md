# Stages 131–140: grounded scene evidence and prerecorded frames

Scoped engineering acceptance and generated measurements do not certify
representative application accuracy or persistent semantic object identity.

## Stage 131

Bounded frame-local topology distinguishes disjoint, touching, overlapping and contained boxes with explicit edge gaps; no semantic identity inference.

Implementation: src/seye/scenes/spatial.py, tests/test_stage131.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage131_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage131_tests.txt.

## Stage 132

Source-normalized boxes, center offsets and distances preserve equivalent scaled layouts while explicitly abstaining without dimensions; not physical perspective invariance or cross-frame registration.

Implementation: src/seye/scenes/spatial.py, tests/test_stage132.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage132_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage132_tests.txt.

## Stage 133

Evidence-linked row/column grouping combines bounded alignment and edge proximity, excluding containment from alignment links; groups are not inferred semantic components.

Implementation: src/seye/scenes/grouping.py, tests/test_stage133.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage133_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage133_tests.txt.

## Stage 134

One-to-one class/box layout similarity exposes alternatives and ambiguity; viewport changes require explicit normalization, which does not prove scene registration or identity.

Implementation: src/seye/scenes/comparison.py, tests/test_stage134.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage134_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage134_tests.txt.

## Stage 135

Source-grid movement/resize/stability evidence records observed and caller-registered residual motion separately; no automatic camera/scroll registration or semantic motion assertion.

Implementation: src/seye/scenes/changes.py, tests/test_stage135.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage135_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage135_tests.txt.
