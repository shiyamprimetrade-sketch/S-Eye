# Stages 101–110: versioned recognition engineering

Scoped engineering acceptance and generated measurements are distinct from
representative application validation. No externally trained model is introduced.

## Stage 101

Versioned geometric edge occupancy, centroid, quadrant and second-moment descriptors with bounded work; not invariant semantic recognition.

Implementation: src/seye/recognition/descriptors.py, src/seye/recognition/pipeline.py, tests/test_stage101.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage101_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage101_tests.txt.

## Stage 102

Versioned RGB histograms, directional texture and spatial luminance layout; color/theme shifts are measured descriptors, not invariance guarantees.

Implementation: src/seye/recognition/descriptors.py, src/seye/recognition/pipeline.py, tests/test_stage102.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage102_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage102_tests.txt.

## Stage 103

Strict schema identity and component-balanced RMS similarity with contribution evidence; similarity is not calibrated confidence.

Implementation: src/seye/recognition/similarity.py, tests/test_stage103.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage103_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage103_tests.txt.

## Stage 104

Schema-bound category means, validation-only radii, split identity/content/group guards and verified checkpoints; independently fitted prototypes, not neural training.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/packet_prototypes.py, tests/test_stage104.py, tests/packet_fixtures.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage104_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage104_tests.txt.

## Stage 105

Checksum/model-bound isotonic empirical correctness support fitted only to the declared validation partition; not representative truth probabilities or guaranteed calibration improvement.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/packet_calibration.py, tests/test_stage105.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage105_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage105_tests.txt.

## Stage 106

Validation-radius and minimum-margin rejection with explicit unknown/ambiguity reasons. Near-prototype unknowns can still be accepted; general open-set recognition is unvalidated.

Implementation: src/seye/recognition/packet_decision.py, tests/test_stage106.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage106_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage106_tests.txt.

## Stage 107

Bounded deterministic candidate ranking exposes alternatives and radius evidence without forcing acceptance of unknowns.

Implementation: src/seye/recognition/packet_decision.py, tests/test_stage107.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage107_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage107_tests.txt.

## Stage 108

Descriptor-difference and component-distance explanations, original source/model/schema hashes and scoped image recognition; labels originate from training, not independently understood semantics.

Implementation: src/seye/recognition/explanation.py, src/seye/integration/service.py, tests/test_stage108.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage108_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage108_tests.txt.
