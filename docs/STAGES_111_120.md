# Stages 111–120: bounded learning and observation persistence

Scoped engineering acceptance and generated measurements are distinct from
representative application validation. No externally trained model is introduced.

## Stage 111

Structural annotation/provenance validation plus a separately versioned centered-edge shape descriptor; translation checks do not certify label truth or general invariance.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/annotations.py, src/seye/recognition/descriptors.py, src/seye/recognition/pipeline.py, tests/test_stage111.py, experiments/stages111_120_protocol.json. Evidence: benchmarks/stage111_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage111_tests.txt.

## Stage 112

Explicit train/validation/test schema, taxonomy and identity/content/group guards for the newly frozen generated protocol; descriptor packets do not certify near-duplicate scene independence.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/partitions.py, scripts/learning_batch_data.py, tests/test_stage112.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage112_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage112_tests.txt.

## Stage 113

Independently seeded normalized-feature SGD with explicit update/loss history, bounded work and validation-only epoch selection; fixed descriptors, not learned visual representations.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/packet_linear.py, tests/test_stage113.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage113_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage113_tests.txt.

## Stage 114

Exclusive integrity-verified selected-weight checkpoints restore exact inference and training metadata; mid-optimizer continuation is explicitly not implemented.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/training_checkpoint.py, tests/test_stage114.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage114_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage114_tests.txt.

## Stage 115

Data/config/source fingerprints and repeated seeded full-model reproduction on one host; different seeds alter initialization, cross-platform byte identity is not promised.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/reproducibility.py, tests/test_stage115.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage115_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage115_tests.txt.

## Stage 116

Strict ordered-history diagnostics flag fixed train/validation accuracy gaps and rising validation loss without touching test data; absence of a warning is not validated generalization.

Implementation: src/seye/learning/diagnostics.py, tests/test_stage116.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage116_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage116_tests.txt.
