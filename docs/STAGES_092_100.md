# Stages 92–100: bounded interface engineering

These are scoped engineering completions. Generated experiments do not establish
real-application accuracy or expert-programmer equivalence. Human review is not
required for engineering continuation. Stage 84/91 comparators are preserved.

## Stage 92

Ordered pixel-cell contours preserve concavity, holes, diagonal separation and signed area; bounded to 200000 boundary edges. Not subpixel/antialiased contour recovery.

Implementation: src/seye/interface/boundaries.py, tests/test_stage092.py, scripts/benchmark_stage92_100.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage092_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage092_tests.txt.

## Stage 93

Conservative inner/outer rectangular boundary pairing with explicit links and source indices; preserves separated/nested regions outside the small-gap rule. Close true nested controls remain ambiguous.

Implementation: src/seye/interface/grouping.py, tests/test_stage093.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage093_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage093_tests.txt.

## Stage 94

Axis projection splitting separates sparse bridges with explicit removed-pixel counts; solid touching/overlapping regions abstain. It is not general object separation.

Implementation: src/seye/interface/masks.py, tests/test_stage094.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage094_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage094_tests.txt.

## Stage 95

Bounded eight-connected area filtering removes isolated specks while preserving qualifying thin strokes; optional policy can remove genuine small glyphs.

Implementation: src/seye/interface/masks.py, tests/test_stage095.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage095_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage095_tests.txt.

## Stage 96

Single-pass bounded axis gap repair records every introduced pixel and warns that distinct regions may join. Large gaps remain unsupported and unchanged.

Implementation: src/seye/interface/masks.py, tests/test_stage096.py. Evidence: benchmarks/stage096_experiment.json and benchmarks/stage096_tests.txt.
